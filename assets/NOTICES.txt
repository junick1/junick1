README asset credits

Photograph: junick, existing junick.dev/public/img/hero.jpg.
Wordmark: Source Serif 4 letter outlines, matching the typography of junick.dev.
Language and tool logos: existing human-made icons from junick.dev/public/img/languages.
The logo trays retain the approved light/dark backgrounds and responsive layouts.
No AI image generation was used. Reference images from wzrabbit were not copied.

Sparkle textures: Kenney Vleugels, Particle Pack (2018), CC0.
https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack
Creator's distribution: https://opengameart.org/content/particle-pack-80-sprites
Files used: star_01.png, star_04.png, star_06.png, circle_05.png.
Changes: resized original raster textures, used luminance as alpha, screen-blended
over the original photograph with staggered opacity/size pulses. 24-frame,
six-second GIF loops at 4 fps. The photograph and typography do not move.
Desktop and mobile crops match the approved preview.
Photo treatment: golden amber, graded from the original JPEG. A warm red/green
balance gives the sky a peach/bronze cast, a contrast curve deepens shadows, and
existing yellow windows shift toward golden orange. Two small highlight bloom
radii derive their glow from the original bright windows. Applied to animated
and still variants. Sparkle textures, placements and timing are unchanged.
No new imagery.

Divider: Kenney Vleugels, Fantasy UI Borders (2023), CC0.
https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-ui-borders
https://kenney-assets.itch.io/fantasy-ui-borders (creator states no generative AI).
File used: PNG/Default/Divider Fade/divider-fade-003.png.
Changes: cropped and extended the premade line, centered its existing ornament,
recoloured neutral gray for light/dark themes; exported as transparent PNG.
This is an adaptation of a premade resource, not an original drawn ornament.
CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
